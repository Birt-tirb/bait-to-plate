# Bait to Plate

A simple fishing companion that keeps your cast button, lures, catch stats and seafood Cooking guide together in a compact interface.

## Fishing made simple

Equip a fishing pole and face the water. On Forever, click **Cast Fishing**, wait for the splash, then click again to reel in. Each cast and catch needs its own click or key press.

Assign **Bait to Plate: Cast / Reel In** under **Keybindings > AddOns**. On Retail and other Classic clients, use this keybinding or right-click the bobber to catch. Their on-screen button casts. WoW's Auto Loot can collect your catch.

Classic and Forever controls appear with a pole equipped. Retail requires Fishing learned and reads the profession tool slot.

## Lures and campfires

The small lure icon beside the fishing button shows your selected lure, how many you have, and its remaining time on your pole. **Right-click to choose a lure, then left-click to apply it.** Forever includes **Camper's Lure** and **"Secret" Bait**, alongside the supported standard pole lures.

The campfire icon uses **right-click to choose, left-click to place**. Forever supports campfire kits, **Fish Bowl**, **Fishing Rack** and **Fishing Hut**. Fishing camp features need a nearby campfire. Other clients use their normal Cooking campfire.

## Your catch journal

See **Session** and **All-Time** totals, catch counts and catch types, first and latest catch artwork, and your five most common catches. All-Time history is saved between sessions and shared across characters on the same WoW account. Session totals restart at login or `/reload`.

Catch stats start hidden after login or `/reload`, while catch tracking continues. Open them through **Toggle Catch Stats** in settings or `/baittoplate stats`. Enable **Show catch stats at startup** if you prefer them open automatically.

## From bag to plate

The **Cooking** tab checks your bags, Cooking skill and known recipes. It covers **25 Forever seafood recipes**, or a **22-recipe Classic seafood guide** on other clients. Retail uses Classic Cooking skill. Expansion recipes are outside this guide.

Recipes you can cook, and recipes using fish you already have, appear first. Hover over a recipe for clear ingredient counts, missing requirements, and where to learn or buy it, including vendor names and locations. Forever's **Mightfish Steak requires an Iron Oven**. Later-client recipe sources that remain unverified are labelled in the tooltip.

Use **Open Cooking** once per character to help verify learned recipes, then craft through WoW's Cooking window at the required station.

## Pools and sound

Enable **Show fishing pools** for **337 known pool and fishable-wreckage locations across 17 Azeroth zones**. Pins appear with a pole equipped. These are known locations, so a pool may be empty. The included data covers Forever and pre-Cataclysm Azeroth, with **no Zephras Isle locations**. The overlay is disabled on Retail and post-Cataclysm clients because their map coordinates differ.

Enable **Louder fishing splash**, then set **Fishing volume** from **0% to 100%**; the default is **100%**. While fishing, Master and Effects use that volume and music and ambience are muted. Other sound effects remain audible. The splash is the game's own sound.

Your previous sound settings return when fishing ends or is interrupted, combat begins, you switch the addon off, log out or reload. Sound changes you make during fishing are preserved.

## Controls and setup

- **Minimap left-click:** show or hide Bait to Plate. Switching it off also pauses catch tracking and its keybinding.
- **Minimap right-click:** open settings.
- **Minimap right-drag:** move the icon.
- **Right-drag the fishing button** or **drag the journal** to reposition them. Positions and the on/off preference are saved.
- Extract the **BankFishing** folder into your client's **Interface/AddOns** folder, enable Bait to Plate, then `/reload` after updates.
