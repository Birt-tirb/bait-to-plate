Bait to Plate fishing-pool data
==============================

PoolData.lua adapts the position records in Wuild's GatherLite 8.4.12
Forever database (4ace5b625a1ee724ca912262509f641b4f35992a).
Records in GatherLite's fishing category were selected, deduplicated by
their map and position, then converted to packed coordinates. This
catalog includes fishable debris and wreckage on established Azeroth
maps. It describes known locations, which can be empty when you visit.

Database: https://github.com/Wuild/GatherLite/blob/4ace5b625a1ee724ca912262509f641b4f35992a/tools/wowhead/forever.json
Fishing catalog: https://github.com/Wuild/GatherLite/blob/4ace5b625a1ee724ca912262509f641b4f35992a/scripts/nodes.lua
Map IDs checked against the Forever 1.60.1.70009 client mapping:
https://github.com/Wuild/GatherLite/blob/4ace5b625a1ee724ca912262509f641b4f35992a/tools/maps/forever-1.60.1.70009/UiMap.csv

GatherLite credits the Wowhead Forever database as its upstream catalog
source. GatherLite's separate navigation code and terrain data are
absent from this adaptation.
